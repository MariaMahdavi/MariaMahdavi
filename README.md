## About Me

I'm a Mathematics and Applications student at Amirkabir University of Technology, interested in numerical analysis and computational methods.

My primary research interest is Scientific Machine Learning, with a background in Numerical Analysis, Partial Differential Equations, and Numerical Linear Algebra. I'm particularly interested in developing and evaluating neural-network-based methods for approximating solutions to physical systems.

## Research & Projects

### 🧮 PINNsFormer Comprehensive Benchmarking

Implementing and rigorously evaluating the PINNsFormer architecture (a Transformer-based Physics-Informed Neural Network with Wavelet activations) against a standard PINN baseline on five diverse PDEs: Burgers', Allen–Cahn, Nonlinear Schrödinger, Kuramoto–Sivashinsky, and Korteweg–de Vries.

**Focus:** Physics-Informed Neural Networks · Transformers · PDEs · Numerical Analysis

📎 [PINNsFormer-PDE-Evaluation](https://github.com/MariaMahdavi/PINNsFormer-PDE-Evaluation)

### 🔧 Adaptive Parameter Tuning and Efficiency Improvements

Developing adaptive schemes for selecting the pseudo-sequence length and time step of PINNsFormer based on each PDE's physical scale, reducing Autograd memory overhead for high-order derivatives, and extending the framework to broader classes of PDEs.

**Technologies:** Python · PyTorch · Automatic Differentiation

### 🔗 Comparative Study of Page Ranking Algorithms

Comparing major page ranking algorithms (PageRank, WPR, HITS, DistanceRank, DirichletRank) for accuracy and efficiency on simulated web graphs, and analyzing the role of link structure in information retrieval.
